The Method

The Simple 3-Part System

A flexible approach designed for real life—not perfection

1

Prep Ingredients, Not Recipes

Instead of cooking five complete meals, prep a few mix-and-match building blocks: proteins, veggies, grains, and sauces. When the parts are ready, dinner becomes assembly—not cooking from scratch.

Example: Baked chicken + chopped veggies + cooked rice + 2 sauces = endless combinations
2

Cook Once, Mix & Match All Week

One prep session turns into multiple meals. Use the same ingredients in different ways so it feels fresh, not repetitive. This saves time, money, and mental energy.

Example: Cooked chicken → tacos Monday, salad Tuesday, rice bowl Thursday
3

Same Meals, New Flavors

Keep it interesting by repeating easy meal formats (bowls, tacos, salads, wraps) but changing the flavor with different sauces, spices, and toppings. Same base, new vibe.

Example: Bowl + teriyaki = Asian night. Bowl + salsa = Tex-Mex night.

The Formula

Your Building Blocks

Prep these basics and you'll have options all week

🍗

3 Proteins

The foundation of every meal. Cook 2-3 proteins and dinner becomes assembly.

  • Rotisserie chicken
  • Baked chicken breast
  • Ground turkey/beef
  • Hard-boiled eggs
  • Beans or lentils
🥦

3 Veggies

Washed, chopped, and ready to use. Fresh + frozen both count!

  • Bell peppers & cucumbers
  • Broccoli florets
  • Cherry tomatoes
  • Leafy greens (washed)
  • Frozen stir-fry blend
🍚

3 Carbs

Whole grains and starches that give meals staying power.

  • Brown rice
  • Quinoa
  • Sweet potatoes
  • Whole grain pasta
  • Tortillas or wraps
🫙

3 Sauces

The secret to variety. Same ingredients, totally different meals.

  • Teriyaki or soy ginger
  • Salsa or chipotle
  • Pesto or Italian
  • Tahini or hummus
  • Simple vinaigrette
